Eighth batch: MSP430 emulated PC-relative displacement (914416)

Interpret the encoded source displacement as a signed 16-bit or 20-bit
value before subtracting the destination PC's extra two bytes. Check the
lower signed bound and mask the result back to its encoded width. This
accepts 0 -> -2 and 1 -> -1, and uses bit 19 rather than bit 15 for MSP430X.
The same defective check existed in non-X emulation and is corrected too.
Architecture reference: TI MSP430x5xx/MSP430x6xx Family User's Guide,
symbolic addressing, https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/slau208/slau208.pdf

Validation: compare RLA/RLC and RLAX/RLCX byte output with explicit ADD/
ADDC expansions for zero, small positive/negative, signed boundary, and
unsigned encoded-negative offsets. Check both lower-bound rejection
cases at each width, and extended offsets crossing bit 15. Full suites
pass in both catalog configurations. Await Coverity rescan confirmation.
